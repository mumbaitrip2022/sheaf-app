## Questions

How do I rotate a PDF and save it on a Mac?

In Preview, select the pages, press Cmd-R and save. In Sheaf, choose Straighten and a fixed copy is saved for you.

How do I rotate only one page?

Use Organize in Sheaf, or select just that thumbnail in Preview and press Cmd-R.

Can I straighten a crooked scan?

Yes. Sheaf’s Straighten levels slightly tilted scanned pages.
