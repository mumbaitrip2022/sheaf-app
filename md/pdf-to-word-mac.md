## What comes across

Sheaf puts the PDF’s text into Word with basic styling (bold, italic, sizes), one page after another. It’s made for getting the words back so you can edit or reuse them: a contract clause, a report, a letter.

It doesn’t rebuild complex layouts. Multi-column magazines, forms and tables may need tidying in Word. For tables of numbers, use [PDF to Excel](https://sheafai.app/bank-statement-pdf-to-excel-mac) instead; for just the text, Convert › Text gives you a plain .txt.

## Scanned PDFs

A scan is a picture of text. Sheaf reads it with macOS’s on-device text recognition before converting, so you get words, not images. To keep the PDF but make it searchable, use [Make searchable](https://sheafai.app/ocr-pdf-mac).
