## Questions

Is there a free alternative to Adobe Acrobat on Mac?

Preview is free and built in for reading, signing and simple merges. Sheaf has a free 7-day trial, then a one-time purchase.

Can I compress a PDF on Mac without Acrobat?

Yes. Preview’s Reduce File Size filter or Sheaf’s Compress both work without Acrobat.

Does Sheaf edit PDF text like Acrobat?

No. Sheaf does whole-document jobs like compress, merge, sign, redact, OCR and convert; it doesn’t edit existing text.
