## Which files work

Word (.docx and .doc), RTF, OpenDocument text (.odt), plain text, Markdown and web pages (.html, .webarchive). Sheaf uses the page size and margins saved in the file, and keeps lists, tables and images in place.

Pages, Keynote and Numbers files aren’t converted by the Mac App Store version; export those from the app itself.

## When to use the app instead

For a document with a complicated design (custom fonts not on your Mac, text boxes, columns), exporting from Word or Pages gives the exact look. Sheaf is the fast way for everyday documents, and for many at once.

## Questions

How do I convert Word to PDF on a Mac without Word?

Drag the .docx onto Sheaf and choose Convert to PDF. TextEdit can also open many Word files and print them to PDF.

Can I convert several Word files to PDF at once?

Yes. Select them all in Finder, drag, and choose Convert to PDF. Each gets its own PDF.

Does it work with Pages files?

Not in the Mac App Store version. In Pages, use File › Export To › PDF.
