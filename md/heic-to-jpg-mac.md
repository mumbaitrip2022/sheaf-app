## Why HEIC causes trouble

HEIC files are about half the size of JPEGs at the same quality, which is why iPhones use them. But many upload forms, older Windows PCs and some websites only accept JPEG or PNG. Converting before you upload avoids “unsupported file” errors.

## Questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG on a Mac for free?

Right-click the photos in Finder › Quick Actions › Convert Image, or open one in Preview and export as JPEG.

Can I convert many HEIC files at once?

Yes. Select them all, drag, and choose Convert › JPEG in Sheaf.

Does converting lose quality?

Slightly, as JPEG is compressed; at normal settings the difference isn’t visible.
