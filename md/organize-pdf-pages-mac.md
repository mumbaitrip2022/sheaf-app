## Questions

How do I rearrange pages in a PDF on a Mac?

Use Preview’s thumbnail sidebar for one file, or Sheaf’s Organize to see a big grid of pages across several PDFs.

How do I delete pages from a PDF on a Mac?

In Organize, select the pages and press Delete, then save. In Preview, select thumbnails and press Delete.

Will the original file change?

Not with Sheaf: it saves a new PDF and keeps the original.
