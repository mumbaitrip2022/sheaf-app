## Merge PDFs and images together

Sheaf accepts PDFs and pictures (JPEG, PNG, HEIC) in the same drag, so a scanned form, two photos of receipts and a signed contract become one PDF. Images are placed on pages sized to fit them.

## Control the order

Files are merged in the order you selected them in Finder. Want to move pages around, drop a blank page or rotate one? Choose [Organize](https://sheafai.app/organize-pdf-pages-mac) instead: you see every page of every file, rearrange them, and save one clean PDF.

## Too big to send after merging?

Drag the merged file again and choose [Compress](https://sheafai.app/compress-pdf-mac). Or split it into parts that fit an email limit.

## Where the files go

## Questions

Can I merge PDFs and JPGs together?

Yes. Sheaf accepts PDFs and images in the same drag and turns them into one PDF.

How do I merge PDFs on a Mac for free?

In Preview, open the first PDF, show thumbnails (View › Thumbnails), drag the other PDFs onto the sidebar, then File › Export as PDF.

Does merging keep bookmarks and links?

Sheaf adds a bookmark for each original file, and the pages keep their text and links.

How do I change the page order before merging?

Use Organize in Sheaf: drag pages into any order across several PDFs, delete or rotate them, then save one file.
