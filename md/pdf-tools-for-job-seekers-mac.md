## Questions

Should I send my résumé as PDF or Word?

PDF, unless the employer asks for Word: it looks the same on every device.

How do I combine my résumé and cover letter into one PDF on a Mac?

Select both, drag, pause and choose Merge.

How do I get my résumé PDF under 2 MB?

Compress it; text-only résumés are usually tiny already. Photos and scanned certificates are what make files big.
