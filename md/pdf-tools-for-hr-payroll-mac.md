# PDF tools for HR and payroll teams

HR handles the most personal documents in a company: payslips, IDs, contracts and tax forms. Sheaf keeps every one of them on the Mac.

**In short:** Sheaf helps HR and payroll teams on Mac password-protect payslips, open locked payslips and tax forms using saved employee details, redact Social Security, National Insurance and other ID numbers, fill and sign onboarding forms, and merge new-starter packs, without uploading employee data.

Updated 9 November 2026

## What it does for you

**Protect pay documents**Add a password to payslips, offer letters and P60s or W-2s before emailing them.

**Open locked payslips**Payroll systems lock payslips with the last characters of the NI number, initials and date of birth, or similar. Saved details open them in one step.

**Redact ID numbers**Finds Social Security, National Insurance, SIN, TFN, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, plus dates of birth, and deletes them.

**Fill and sign forms**Type into onboarding and tax forms, sign them, and lock the answers in.

**New-starter packs**Merge the contract, handbook and forms into one PDF with bookmarks.

**Remove hidden data**Strip comments and edit history from contracts before they go out.

## A typical day

1. Drag the month's payslips onto Sheaf and choose Protect.
2. When an employee sends ID, drag it and choose Redact to remove numbers you don't need to keep.
3. Drag the onboarding pack and choose Merge.

Yes. Redact finds UK National Insurance numbers, US Social Security numbers and other national ID formats and deletes the text.
