## Exactly where it belongs

Pages scroll vertically, and you can place a signature on each page that needs one, at the size you want. For a stack of documents that all need a signature in the same place, sign them together.

## Is it legally binding?

In many countries a simple electronic signature is valid for everyday agreements like leases, offer letters and permission slips, but rules vary by country and document type. For regulated documents (some real estate, court or government forms), check what the other party requires; some need a certified digital signature service.
