How it works

Fill PDF forms in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Choose More › Fill form

    Type the answers, then save, or lock them so they can’t change.

Your options on a Mac

Three ways to do it, honestly compared.

Preview (built in)

Click a field and type; save.

  • Free and built in
  • Types into form fields
  • Answers stay editable

Online PDF sites

Upload the file to a website, wait, download the result.

  • Your document goes to someone else’s server
  • Free plans limit files, size or tasks per day
  • Needs an internet connection

SheafFastest

Drag the file, pause, let go.

  • Type into form fields
  • Lock the answers into the page
  • Sign the completed copy

Lock the answers

A filled form usually stays editable: anyone can change an answer. Choosing to lock (flatten) the answers draws them into the page, which is what many offices and courts ask for.

Forms without fields

Scanned forms have no fields to type into. Print-and-scan is the fallback, or ask the sender for a fillable version.

Then sign

Finish by signing the PDF, and compress it if it must be uploaded.

Questions

How do I fill out a PDF form on a Mac?

Open it in Preview and click the fields, or drag it onto Sheaf and choose Fill form.

How do I make a filled form uneditable?

Choose to lock the answers in Sheaf; they’re drawn into the page.

Why can’t I type into my PDF form?

It may be a scan with no form fields. Ask for a fillable version, or print and scan it.
