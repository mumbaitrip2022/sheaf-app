How it works

Watermark in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Choose More › Watermark

    Pick Draft or Confidential, or type your own words.

Questions

Can I add a watermark in Preview?

No. Preview has no watermark tool.

Can a watermark be removed?

Sheaf draws it into the page, so it can’t be clicked away. Determined people with editing tools can still alter any PDF.

Can I use my own words?

Yes. Type any text, like your company name or “Copy for review”.
