How it works

Word to PDF in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Let go on Convert to PDF

    A PDF appears next to each document, ready to send.

Your options on a Mac

Three ways to do it, honestly compared.

Word or Pages

Open the file, then File › Export / Save as PDF.

  • Exact layout from the app itself
  • One file at a time
  • Needs the app installed

Online PDF sites

Upload the file to a website, wait, download the result.

  • Your document goes to someone else’s server
  • Free plans limit files, size or tasks per day
  • Needs an internet connection

SheafFastest

Drag the file, pause, let go.

  • Many documents at once
  • .docx, .doc, .rtf, .odt, .txt, .md, .html
  • No Word or Pages needed

Which files work

Word (.docx and .doc), RTF, OpenDocument text (.odt), plain text, Markdown and web pages (.html, .webarchive). Sheaf uses the page size and margins saved in the file, and keeps lists, tables and images in place.

Pages, Keynote and Numbers files aren’t converted by the Mac App Store version; export those from the app itself.

When to use the app instead

For a document with a complicated design (custom fonts not on your Mac, text boxes, columns), exporting from Word or Pages gives the exact look. Sheaf is the fast way for everyday documents, and for many at once.

After converting

Combine the PDFs into one with Merge, add page numbers, or remove the author name before sending.

Questions

How do I convert Word to PDF on a Mac without Word?

Drag the .docx onto Sheaf and choose Convert to PDF. TextEdit can also open many Word files and print them to PDF.

Can I convert several Word files to PDF at once?

Yes. Select them all in Finder, drag, and choose Convert to PDF. Each gets its own PDF.

Does it work with Pages files?

Not in the Mac App Store version. In Pages, use File › Export To › PDF.
