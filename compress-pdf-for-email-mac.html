How big is too big?

Personal Gmail accounts can attach up to 25 MB per message; anything larger is turned into a Google Drive link (Google’s help page). Other providers and company mail servers set their own limits, and many are lower, so check yours.

Attachments grow by about a third when they’re encoded for email, and the whole message counts, so aim a little under the limit.

Why not use a cloud link?

Links work, but the receiver needs to open another site, links expire, and some offices block them. A file under the limit just arrives.

Uploading to a portal instead?

Government and job portals often allow far less, sometimes 2–10 MB. See PDF size limits for IRS, USCIS, ATO and other portals, and use Fit 5 MB.

Questions

How do I reduce a PDF to under 25 MB on a Mac?

Drag it, pause, choose Compress › Fit 25 MB in Sheaf. In Preview, Export with the Reduce File Size filter and check the result.

What if the PDF is still too big after compressing?

Split it into email-sized parts with Split › By size and send them in separate emails, or share a link instead.

Why is my PDF so large?

Usually scanned pages or photos saved at full resolution. Compressing shrinks those images and keeps text sharp.
