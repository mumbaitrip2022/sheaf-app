How it works

JPG to PDF in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Let go on Make PDF

    One PDF appears, a page per image, in the order you selected them.

Your options on a Mac

Three ways to do it, honestly compared.

Preview (built in)

Open all images, select the thumbnails, File › Print › Save as PDF.

  • Free and built in
  • Several steps
  • Easy to get the order wrong

Online PDF sites

Upload the file to a website, wait, download the result.

  • Your document goes to someone else’s server
  • Free plans limit files, size or tasks per day
  • Needs an internet connection

Questions

How do I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF on a Mac?

Select them in Finder, drag, pause, and choose Make PDF in Sheaf. In Preview, open them together and print to PDF.

Can I convert HEIC photos to PDF?

Yes. Sheaf accepts HEIC, JPEG and PNG in the same drag.

Why is my photo PDF so large?

Phone photos are high resolution. Compress the PDF afterwards to shrink it.
