Questions

Is there an iLovePDF app for Mac?

iLovePDF lists a desktop app for Mac and Windows with its Premium plan.

Why does iLovePDF say I’ve reached my limit?

Its free plan limits processing per task. An app that works on your Mac has no such limits.

Can I compress a PDF on Mac without uploading it?

Yes: Preview’s Reduce File Size filter or Sheaf’s Compress both work on your Mac.
