Why not just use a free website?

Free PDF sites cap the number of files or tasks per day, and they upload your work and your ID scans to their servers. Sheaf works with Wi-Fi off, in the library or on a train, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Questions

Is there a free PDF app for Mac students?

Preview is free and built into macOS. Sheaf has a free 7-day trial and then 3 free actions a day; unlimited use is a one-time purchase.

How do I make my assignment PDF smaller for Canvas or Moodle?

Drag the PDF and choose Compress › Fit 5 MB or Fit 25 MB, whichever your course allows.

How do I turn photos of handwritten work into one PDF?

Select the photos, drag them, and choose Make PDF. Then Compress if it’s large.
