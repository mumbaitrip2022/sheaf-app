PDF tools for clinics and healthcare offices

Referrals, scanned records and insurance forms carry patient data. Sheaf works on them without sending them anywhere.

In short: Sheaf helps clinics and healthcare offices on Mac redact patient names, medical record numbers, NHS and Medicare numbers and dates of birth, make scanned referrals searchable, compress scans for email and password-protect files, with everything processed on the Mac. Sheaf doesn't upload files, which keeps patient data off third-party servers; your own privacy obligations still apply.

A typical day

  1. Drag a scanned referral and choose Make searchable.
  2. Drag it again and choose Redact to remove identifiers before sharing it for research or audit.
  3. Choose Protect to add a password.

Why it's safe to use with client files

  • Nothing is uploaded. Every action runs on the Mac; Sheaf has no servers and no account.
  • Originals are never changed. Each result is a new file next to the original, with Undo.
  • Sandboxed and reviewed by Apple. Sheaf is sold only on the Mac App Store and opens only the files you give it.
  • Ready for IT. Sheaf for Business deploys through Apple Business Manager with managed settings.

Questions

Is Sheaf HIPAA compliant?

HIPAA applies to how your organization handles data, not to an app on its own. Sheaf processes files only on the Mac and has no servers, so it doesn't store or transmit patient data.

Does redaction work on scanned documents?

Yes. Sheaf reads the scanned text on the Mac, finds identifiers and deletes them from the new file.
