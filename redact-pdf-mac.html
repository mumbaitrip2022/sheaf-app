Why black boxes fail

If you cover text with a shape in a PDF editor, the original text usually stays in the file. Anyone can select it, copy it, or delete the shape. This has leaked court filings and government documents more than once.

What Sheaf finds

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment card numbers, bank account numbers and national ID formats (like US Social Security numbers, UK National Insurance numbers, Australian TFNs and others), including in scanned pages it reads with on-device text recognition. You can add your own words too.

Also remove what you can’t see

A PDF can carry the author’s name, edit history, comments and attachments. Before sending a redacted file outside, remove hidden data too.

Questions

Can I redact in Preview?

Preview has a Redact tool, but you mark each item yourself. Sheaf finds names, emails and numbers for you and removes the text underneath.

Can redacted text be recovered?

Not from a properly redacted file: the text is removed, not hidden. Keep the original somewhere safe if you need it later.

Does redaction work on scanned PDFs?

Yes. Sheaf reads scanned pages with on-device text recognition, so it can find and remove details in scans too.

Is it safe to redact PDFs online?

You would be uploading the very information you want to hide. Redact on your Mac instead.
