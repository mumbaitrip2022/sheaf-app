How to choose

Pick by the job, not the brand.

Quick jobs, many times a day

Compress, merge, sign, convert: Sheaf does them where you drag the file. Preview handles the basics for free.

Editing the words in a PDF

Changing paragraphs or building fillable forms is Acrobat’s and PDF Expert’s home turf. Sheaf doesn’t edit existing text.

Free is the priority

Preview is built in. PDFgear is free with more tools. Sheaf keeps 3 actions a day free after its trial.

Mac, Windows and phone

UPDF and Acrobat cover every platform with one account. Sheaf is Mac-only.

Private documents

Contracts, IDs, statements: use an app that works on your Mac rather than uploading to a website.

No subscription

Sheaf is one purchase. PDF Expert and UPDF also sell lifetime licences. Acrobat and Smallpdf are subscriptions.

Sheaf

A menu bar app: start dragging a PDF, pause, and actions open around your cursor. Compress, merge, split, sign, redact, OCR, convert, page numbers, watermarks, PDF to Excel and more, each in about a second, all on the Mac. Not for: editing existing text, annotating while you read, Windows or iPhone.

Apple Preview

Free and already installed. It reads PDFs well and can merge (via thumbnails), sign, fill forms, rotate, redact by hand and reduce file size with a filter that often blurs text. Not for: page numbers, watermarks, OCR you can save, Word conversion.

Adobe Acrobat

The most complete PDF editor, with text editing, form building and e-signature workflows. It’s a subscription: Standard is US$14.99 a month on an annual plan, Pro US$19.99 (Adobe’s US prices). Overkill if you mainly compress, merge and sign.

PDF Expert

A polished Mac reader and editor from Readdle, sold as a yearly plan or a one-time Lifetime licence for Mac, on the Mac App Store. Strong for reading, annotating and editing.

PDFgear

Free, with a long tool list and AI features (“PDFgear AI”), for Mac and the web. Its site doesn’t say which features run on your Mac and which online.

UPDF

An editor for Mac, Windows, iOS and Android under one account, with yearly or lifetime licences. The free version adds trial watermarks to edited, annotated or protected files; AI features are an extra subscription.

Smallpdf and iLovePDF

Popular browser tools: nothing to install, so they’re handy on a borrowed computer. You upload the file to their servers, and free use is limited. Smallpdf is billed annually per user; iLovePDF has a free plan with per-task limits and a Premium plan that adds a desktop app.

Questions

What is the best free PDF app for Mac?

Preview, built into macOS, covers reading, signing, simple merges and form filling. PDFgear is free with more tools. Sheaf’s trial is free for 7 days, then 3 actions a day stay free.

What is the best Adobe Acrobat alternative for Mac?

It depends on the job: PDF Expert or UPDF for editing, Sheaf for fast everyday tasks without a subscription, Preview for the basics for free.

Which PDF apps work offline on Mac?

Preview and Sheaf run on your Mac. Smallpdf and iLovePDF’s web tools need the internet and upload files. Check other apps’ sites for which features need a connection.

Is there a PDF app for Mac without a subscription?

Yes: Sheaf is a one-time purchase, Preview and PDFgear are free, and PDF Expert and UPDF sell lifetime licences.
