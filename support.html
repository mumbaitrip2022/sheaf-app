How do I use Sheaf?

Start dragging a PDF, image or document and pause for a moment. A circle of actions opens around the file. Move toward one (Compress, Merge, Convert…) and let go. The result appears right there, and every result can be undone. You can also click the Sheaf icon in the menu bar to choose files.

Why are new files saved to Downloads?

Mac App Store apps can only open the files you give them. To save results next to your originals, open Sheaf Settings › Results › Folder access, click Allow… and choose your home folder. Sheaf never uploads your files.

How does the free trial work?

Sheaf is free to download. Start the 7-day free trial to use everything, unlimited; no payment is needed. After the trial you keep 3 free actions a day. Sheaf Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything for good, with Family Sharing. There is no subscription.

I bought Sheaf Pro. How do I restore it on another Mac?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Sheaf Settings and click Restore under Sheaf Pro.

The Finder shortcut doesn’t work

The first time you press it (⌃⌥S by default), macOS asks whether Sheaf may read your Finder selection. If you declined, turn it on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation › Sheaf › Finder.
