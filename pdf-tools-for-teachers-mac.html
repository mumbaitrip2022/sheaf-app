A typical week

From pile of files to done.

  1. Plan

    Drag this week’s worksheets together, choose Organize, put them in order and save one pack.

  2. Prepare

    Number the pages and watermark the answer key “Teacher copy”.

  3. Share

    Compress the newsletter, redact names from the example essay, and send.

Student privacy

Schools have good reasons to be careful with student work and names. Sheaf never uploads files, has no account and collects no data, so using it doesn’t add another service that holds student information.

Questions

Is Sheaf safe for student data?

Files never leave the Mac. Sheaf has no servers, no account and collects no data.

Can our school deploy it?

Yes. Sheaf for Business is available through Apple School Manager and works with any MDM.

How do I combine worksheets into one PDF on a Mac?

Select them in Finder, drag, pause and choose Merge, or Organize to set the order first.
