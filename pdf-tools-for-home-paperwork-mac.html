Questions

How do I scan documents to PDF on a Mac without a scanner?

Photograph them with your iPhone, drag the photos into Sheaf and choose Make PDF. Continuity Camera can also scan straight into Finder.

Can my family share one purchase?

Yes. Sheaf supports Family Sharing.

How do I make a PDF small enough for a tax or visa portal?

Choose Compress › Fit 5 MB, or the limit your portal lists.
