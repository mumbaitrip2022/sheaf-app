How it works

Sign PDF in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Let go on Sign

    Drag your signature where it belongs. Choose first page, last page or every page.

Sign once, reuse forever

Draw your signature with the trackpad or mouse the first time. Sheaf remembers it on this Mac, so next time signing takes one drag. It’s stored locally and never uploaded.

Exactly where it belongs

Pages scroll vertically, and you can place a signature on each page that needs one, at the size you want. For a stack of documents that all need a signature in the same place, sign them together.

Is it legally binding?

In many countries a simple electronic signature is valid for everyday agreements like leases, offer letters and permission slips, but rules vary by country and document type. For regulated documents (some real estate, court or government forms), check what the other party requires; some need a certified digital signature service.

Fill in the form first

If the PDF has form fields, fill them before signing, then sign the completed copy.

Where is my signature stored?

Can I sign a PDF on Mac without Adobe?

Yes. Preview’s Markup › Sign and Sheaf both sign PDFs without Adobe Acrobat or an account.

Can I sign several PDFs at once?

Yes. Drag them together and choose Sign; the signature goes in the same place on each.
