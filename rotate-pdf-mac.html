How it works

Rotate PDF in three moves.

No app to open, no window to find. It happens where the file already is.

  1. Drag

    Start dragging the file from Finder, Mail, Photos or your desktop.

  2. Pause

    Hold still for a moment. Sheaf’s actions open in a circle around your cursor.

  3. Let go on Straighten

    Sideways pages turn upright; tilted scans are levelled. A fixed copy appears.

Questions

How do I rotate a PDF and save it on a Mac?

In Preview, select the pages, press Cmd-R and save. In Sheaf, choose Straighten and a fixed copy is saved for you.

How do I rotate only one page?

Use Organize in Sheaf, or select just that thumbnail in Preview and press Cmd-R.

Can I straighten a crooked scan?

Yes. Sheaf’s Straighten levels slightly tilted scanned pages.
