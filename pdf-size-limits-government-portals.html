PDF size limits for government upload portals

Tax and immigration portals reject files over their limit, often after you've filled in the whole form. Here are the limits for the most-used portals, and how to get under them.

In short: Common per-file limits are 15 MB for the IRS Documentation Upload Tool, 12 MB for USCIS, 6 MB for the ATO's online services, 5 MB for India's Income Tax and GST portals and 5 MB for myGov Child Support. To get a PDF under a limit on a Mac, drag it onto Sheaf and choose Compress › Fit, which compresses until the file is under the size and keeps text sharp.

Get a PDF under the limit

  1. Start dragging the PDFs, pause and choose Compress.
  2. Choose Fit with your portal's size, for example Fit 5 MB.
  3. Sheaf compresses each file until it's under the limit and keeps text sharp. Upload the copies, marked “upload”.

Sheaf sets the default size from your country: 10 MB in the US, 5 MB in India and Australia. Change it in Settings › Upload limit.

Questions

What is the IRS upload file size limit?

The IRS Documentation Upload Tool accepts JPEG, PNG and PDF files up to 15 MB each.

What is the USCIS file size limit?

USCIS online filing accepts files up to 12 MB each for most forms. Check the form's upload page.

What is the ATO attachment size limit?

ATO online services for business and agents accept up to 6 attachments per message, each up to 6 MB.

How do I reduce a PDF to 5 MB on a Mac?

Drag it onto Sheaf and choose Compress › Fit 5 MB. If it can't get that small without hurting quality, Split makes parts under the limit.
